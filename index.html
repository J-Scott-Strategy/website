FAQ

Common questions

What does a RevOps consultant do?

I help sales, marketing, and operations teams run on one set of systems and numbers. That usually means cleaning up and configuring the CRM, connecting it to the rest of the tech stack, and building the reporting finance and leadership use to run the business.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Mainly HubSpot and Salesforce. I handle audits, new implementations, migrations between the two, and integrations with tools like Clay, your data warehouse, and your marketing and support tools.

Why use Google BigQuery as a data warehouse?

BigQuery is fully managed and serverless, so there's nothing to maintain, and it scales from a few gigabytes to petabytes without rebuilding anything. You pay for storage and the queries you run, which keeps costs reasonable for growing companies. It also connects directly to most BI tools and data pipelines.

What is an AI data agent?

An AI data agent lets people ask questions about company data in plain English, like "What was pipeline by source last quarter?", and get an answer without writing SQL or waiting on an analyst. It works best on a well-modeled warehouse with a clear data dictionary, so answers stay consistent.

Do you work with companies outside Kansas City?

Yes. I'm based in Kansas City and work with clients remotely across the US and internationally.

How does an engagement start?

With a short call about your systems and goals. From there I usually recommend an audit of your CRM, data, and reporting, followed by a phased plan. Work can be a fixed-scope project or ongoing fractional support.
