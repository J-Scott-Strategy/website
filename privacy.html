Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

J. Scott Strategy is the business name of Jordan Scott, an independent consultant based in Kansas City, Missouri, United States. This policy explains what information J. Scott Strategy ("we," "us") collects when you visit jscottstrategy.com or contact us through it, how we use that information, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the contact form, we collect your first and last name, email address, company, phone number, the type of help you're looking for, and your message. Only your first name and email are required.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, we and our service providers collect:

  • Pages you view, how you arrived at the site, and the time and date of your visit
  • Browser and device type
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as city and country)

This is collected through cookies and similar technologies described below.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about a potential engagement
  • To provide and manage services if we work together
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve it
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The site uses HubSpot to manage contact form submissions and to understand site traffic. HubSpot sets cookies (including hubspotutk, __hstc, __hssc, and __hssrc) that recognize your browser across visits and connect your page views to your form submission if you contact us.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, though we won't be able to connect your visits to your inquiry.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and our business. They process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services:

  • HubSpot, for contact form submissions, our CRM, and site analytics
  • Google, for email (Google Workspace) and for loading the fonts used on the site (Google Fonts), which receives your IP address when the page loads
  • GitHub, which hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security and reliability

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a merger, sale, or transfer of the business.

How long we keep information

We keep contact and inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you, manage any working relationship, and meet legal and business record-keeping needs. When it's no longer needed, we delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Delete your information
  • Stop sending you follow-up emails

Depending on where you live, including certain U.S. states, the European Union, and the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights under local law, such as the right to object to or restrict processing, or to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. We will honor requests as required by applicable law.

To make a request, use the contact form and include "Privacy request" in your message. You can also unsubscribe from any email we send by replying to it.

International visitors

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using the site, you understand that your information may be processed outside your home country.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we collect, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access controls on the systems we use. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

The site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form on this site.
